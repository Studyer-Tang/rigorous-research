# Mathematical audit

## Definitions before calculations

Rewrite the dependency chain from ambient object to target expression. Check type correctness at every step. For presented algebraic objects, enumerate all generator families, relations, index ranges, coefficient assumptions, and inclusions or transition maps.

If a printed relation contains an unavailable generator, the literal quotient may be undefined. A natural repair is a new statement requiring its own label and justification; naturalness alone does not establish authorial intent.

## Constructions and representations

For a proposed witness or representation, verify in this order:

1. matrices or maps have the claimed domain and codomain;
2. parameters lie in a ring where every used inverse exists;
3. all defining relations hold, not only the convenient subset;
4. the assignment descends through every quotient used;
5. the target element has been translated without convention or ordering errors;
6. nonvanishing is proved in the actual ring or module, not a larger informal expression space.

A representation showing the image of an element is nonzero proves the element is nonzero only after the representation is shown to be well-defined on the exact algebra under discussion.

## Equality and nonvanishing

Name the normal form, basis, faithful action, specialization, invariant, or coefficient argument that decides equality. If generic parameters are used, list the exceptional locus. Distinguish polynomial identity, rational-function identity, and equality after specialization.

For a counterexample, verify that the witness satisfies every hypothesis. For an existence proof, verify that the construction is an object in the required category and not merely in a completion, extension, or limit unless permitted.

## Stable families and limits

Check transition maps explicitly. A statement at every finite level, a statement in a direct limit, and a uniformly compatible family are different claims. Determine whether relations added at later levels can kill an earlier element. An element nonzero before passing to the limit need not remain nonzero without injectivity or another persistence argument.

## Manual verification format

Display the decisive derivation as small equalities with the invoked definition or relation beside each step. Keep convention-sensitive expansions explicit. Use independent recomputation or a second method for the step on which nonzero, injective, or exhaustive behavior rests.
