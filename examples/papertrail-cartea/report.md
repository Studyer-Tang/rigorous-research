# The Limited Virtue of Complexity - PaperTrail experiment

This public experiment tests PaperTrail against a 119-page finance preprint. It intentionally includes one appropriately qualified claim, one overgeneralized negative control, and one figure-specific empirical claim. The source PDF itself is not redistributed.

## Claims

- [C001] The benefits of model complexity depend on data quality and infrastructure; noise can eventually overwhelm the value of adding features. [@cartea2025]
- [C002] More model complexity always improves return prediction and portfolio performance. [@cartea2025]
- [C003] In the paper's Figure 5 experiment, out-of-sample R-squared and Sharpe ratio rise from low to moderate feature dimensions and then decline at higher dimensions. [@cartea2025]

## Experiment limits

- The source is an SSRN preprint (`posted-content` in Crossref and `preprint` in OpenAlex), not a verified journal version of record.
- The empirical sample is restricted to selected U.S. equities and the paper's stated forecasting design; it does not establish a universal result for all markets, investors, or models.
- Included excerpts are AI-assisted extraction drafts. They remain `UNREVIEWED` until a person checks them against the PDF in the browser review desk.
