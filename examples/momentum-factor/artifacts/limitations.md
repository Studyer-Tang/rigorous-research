# Interpretation firewall

The analysis supports, at most, a fixed-period descriptive result plus a conditional weak-dependence inference for the published factor series.

It does not establish:

- direct tradability of the underlying long-short portfolios;
- performance net of spread, impact, financing, borrow, taxes, or implementation delay;
- an alpha relative to a separately estimated factor model;
- a causal risk premium;
- persistence after December 2024;
- immunity to revisions of the source series.

The downloaded archive's SHA-256 digest and available date range are recorded in the analysis artifact. If the provider revises history, the new run must be treated as a new data vintage and compared explicitly rather than silently replacing the evidence.
