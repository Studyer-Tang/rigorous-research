# Problem and contract

For an integer \(n\ge 1\), let

\[
T_n(\rho)=\bigl(\rho^{|i-j|}\bigr)_{1\le i,j\le n}\in M_n(\mathbb Z[\rho]).
\]

The claim is the exact polynomial identity

\[
\det T_n(\rho)=(1-\rho^2)^{n-1}.
\]

The matrix, determinant, and powers are defined over the commutative polynomial ring \(\mathbb Z[\rho]\). Consequently, a proof in this ring specializes to every commutative ring after choosing an image of \(\rho\). No division by \(1-\rho^2\) is permitted in the proof, because that would silently exclude the singular specializations \(\rho=\pm1\).
