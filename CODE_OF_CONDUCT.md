# Code of conduct

We want participation in Rigorous Research to be safe, constructive, and technically honest.

Participants must:

- treat people with respect across differences in experience, identity, discipline, and viewpoint;
- critique claims, evidence, code, and methods rather than attacking contributors;
- disclose conflicts of interest and avoid presenting fabricated, private, or improperly licensed material as evidence;
- accept negative and inconclusive findings when they follow from the documented release gates;
- stop publishing sensitive details when a security, privacy, or research-participant risk is identified.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate falsification, credential exposure, and retaliation are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or report serious conduct to the relevant platform or authority.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact or security-advisory channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
